# Browse page art provenance

## Generated card illustrations

Every card illustration on the Library (`/browse`) page, including the Come, Follow Me card, was created with the
built-in `image_gen` tool, one call per asset, without source-image inputs. The first seven were created on 2026-10-24;
the Come, Follow Me footprints image was generated later as a symbolic replacement for the previously used Heinrich Hofmann
portrait, which is no longer shipped.
These are original decorative illustrations; no Creative Commons license or historical reconstruction is claimed.
No people, Savior figures, angels, or deity depictions were generated.

All eight files were mechanically resized to 640 × 640 (center crop) and encoded as WebP at quality 80 using `sharp`;
no generative changes were made after creation. Full final prompts, filenames, byte sizes and SHA-256 checksums
are retained in [browse-generated-art-prompts.json](browse-generated-art-prompts.json).
