F1, P2 — Layout and spacing; accessibility; states and feedback. Location: mobile Genesis 1 reader, light and dark, 390 × 844. Evidence: E7–E8. User impact: the bottom navigation covers the tip's lower text while the translucent tip also covers the reading text; readers may miss the instruction or need to work around overlapping layers. Proposed change: place the tip fully above the bottom navigation with sufficient reserved space, or use a non-overlapping in-flow treatment that remains readable in both themes. Preserve the warm serif reading presentation. Verification: capture both themes at 320 and 390 px, verify the complete tip and dismiss action are visible, navigate by keyboard, dismiss it, and scroll through the final verse without obscured content. Tradeoff: reserves some vertical reading space while the tip is present. Raised by: this reviewer; dissent: none available.
