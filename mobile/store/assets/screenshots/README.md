# Native Android screenshots

These five screenshots were supplied by Isaac from his phone in the release-preparation thread on 2026-10-27. They depict the 1.0.2 preview, whose in-app UI also ships in 1.0.3. They are real captures, copied byte-for-byte: no crops, generation, resizing, alpha removal, or UI reconstruction. The new 1.0.3 launcher and splash are not pictured.

Google Play Console accepted the original 896×2000 PNGs into the phone screenshot slots and advanced the complete listing through validation to **Ready to send for review** on 2026-10-27. This is Console validation, not a Google policy-review approval. The UI guidance recommends 9:16 or 16:9; these originals are taller and narrower, and not eligible for the 1080-pixel promotional placements. If review later requests another format, capture or deterministically format new originals with the owner.

`manifest.json` records attachment identifiers and SHA-256 hashes. The Browse screenshot contains generated illustration assets, and the Come, Follow Me screenshots contain AI-authored guide material. Those screenshot assets, plus the generated feature graphic, received asset-specific AI labels in Play Console. These labels do not mean the app captures were fabricated.
